# 503A option title clarification

FDA's “Compounding and the FDA: Questions and Answers” explicitly states: “Compounded drugs are not FDA-approved.” It describes licensed pharmacy/physician compounding and patient-specific needs; 503A is not approval of the resulting products. The user-provided description correctly frames licensed 503A pharmacy partners and valid patient-specific prescriptions, but the title “503a FDA Approved Products and Medical Supplies” could incorrectly imply approval of compounded medications. Requested permission to replace only the title with “503A Compounded Medications and Medical Supplies” before deployment.

Source: https://www.fda.gov/drugs/human-drug-compounding/compounding-and-fda-questions-and-answers
Verified November 10, 2026.
